# Vrinda Store Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive E-Commerce Sales Dashboard developed using Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, customer orders, product categories, and sales trends. The dashboard provides meaningful insights through charts, KPI cards, and interactive slicers.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview
![Vrinda Store Sales Dashboard](dashboard.png)

## Objectives

- Analyze total sales amount and total orders.
- Track monthly sales performance and trends.
- Analyze order status, including delivered, cancelled, returned, and refunded orders.
- Compare sales and orders across different e-commerce channels.
- Identify the top 5 states based on sales.
- Compare sales distribution between men and women.
- Analyze orders by age group and gender.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Amount:** 2,11,76,377
- **Total Sales/Orders:** 31,047

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Data Visualization
- Dashboard Design

## Dashboard Features

- Interactive monthly filters.
- Product category and sales channel filters.
- Order status analysis.
- Channel-wise order distribution.
- Gender-wise sales comparison.
- State-wise sales analysis.
- Monthly Orders vs. Sales visualization.
- Age group and gender analysis.

## Key Insights

- Delivered orders account for approximately 92% of orders.
- Amazon contributes the highest share of orders at 35%.
- Women account for 64% of sales, compared with 36% for men.
- Maharashtra is among the leading states in sales, with approximately 2,990.2M shown in the dashboard.
- Adult customers represent a major share of orders across both genders.

## Conclusion

This dashboard helps transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful insights. It enables users to monitor sales performance, understand customer purchasing patterns, evaluate sales channels, and support data-driven business decisions.
